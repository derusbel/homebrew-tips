

	tar
	
	Packs many files into one bundle, and unpacks them again.
	More information: https://manned.org/tar.


		- Unpack a bundle:
		tar -xf bundle.tar.gz

		- Pack a folder into a bundle:
		tar -czf bundle.tar.gz folder/

		- Peek inside without unpacking:
		tar -tf bundle.tar.gz


	 Remember "x" for extract, and "c" for create.
