

	head
	
	Shows you the beginning of a file.
	More information: https://manned.org/head.


		- See the first 10 lines:
		head file.txt

		- See the first 3 lines:
		head -n 3 file.txt

		- See the beginning of several files:
		head file1.txt file2.txt


	 For the end of a file instead, see the "tail" page.
