

	cut
	
	Takes one piece out of every line.
	Handy for lists and spreadsheet files.
	More information: https://manned.org/cut.


		- Take the first column of a comma list:
		cut -d , -f 1 list.csv

		- Take the second and third columns:
		cut -d , -f 2,3 list.csv

		- Take the first 10 letters of every line:
		cut -c 1-10 file.txt
