

	ln
	
	Makes a shortcut to a file or folder.
	More information: https://manned.org/ln.


		- Make a shortcut:
		ln -s path/to/original shortcut

		- Make a shortcut inside another folder:
		ln -s path/to/original path/to/folder/


	 The "s" makes a normal shortcut, which is the one you almost always want.
