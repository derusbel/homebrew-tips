

	jobs
	
	Lists the things you left running in the background of this terminal.
	More information: https://manned.org/jobs.


		- See what is running in the background:
		jobs

		- Bring job number 1 back to the front:
		fg %1

		- Send job number 1 to keep going in the background:
		bg %1


	 Press "control + z" to push whatever is running into the background.
