

	less
	
	Reads a long file one screen at a time,
	so it does not all rush past you.
	More information: https://manned.org/less.


		- Open a file to read it:
		less file.txt

		- Open it with line numbers:
		less -N file.txt

		- Open it and keep the colors:
		less -R file.txt


	 Move with the arrow keys. Search by typing "/" and a word. Quit with "q".
