

	mdfind
	
	Searches your whole machine, the same way the search bar does.
	More information: https://manned.org/mdfind.


		- Search everywhere for a word:
		mdfind "holiday"

		- Search by file name only:
		mdfind -name "notes.txt"

		- Search inside one folder:
		mdfind -onlyin path/to/folder "holiday"
