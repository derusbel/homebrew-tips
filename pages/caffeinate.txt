

	caffeinate
	
	Stops your machine falling asleep.
	More information: https://manned.org/caffeinate.


		- Keep it awake until you stop this:
		caffeinate

		- Keep it awake for one hour:
		caffeinate -t 3600

		- Keep it awake until a command finishes:
		caffeinate brew upgrade


	 Press "control + c" to let it sleep again.
