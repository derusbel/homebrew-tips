

	alias
	
	Gives a long command a short nickname.
	More information: https://manned.org/alias.


		- Make a nickname for this session:
		alias ll="ls -l -a"

		- See all the nicknames you have:
		alias

		- Forget a nickname:
		unalias ll


	 Nicknames disappear when you close the terminal. To keep one, put the line in your ~/.zshrc file.
