

	tmux
	
	Splits your terminal into several, and keeps them running
	even if you close the window.
	More information: https://manned.org/tmux.


		- Start a session with a name:
		tmux new -s work

		- Leave it running and step away:
		tmux detach

		- Come back to it later:
		tmux attach -t work

		- See the sessions you left open:
		tmux ls


	 If you do not have it yet: brew install tmux.
