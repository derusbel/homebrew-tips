

	killall
	
	Closes every copy of a program, by name instead of by number.
	More information: https://manned.org/killall.
				[38;2;255;66;69m
	Warning: *This closes every window of that program at once, saved or not.*
				[0m


		- Close a program by name:
		killall "Safari"

		- Force it closed:
		killall -9 "Safari"
