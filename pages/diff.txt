

	diff
	
	Shows what is different between two files.
	More information: https://manned.org/diff.


		- Compare two files:
		diff file1.txt file2.txt

		- Compare them side by side:
		diff -y file1.txt file2.txt

		- Just say whether they are the same:
		diff -q file1.txt file2.txt


	 No answer at all means the files are identical.
