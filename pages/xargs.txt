

	xargs
	
	Takes a list of things and feeds them to another command, one by one.
	More information: https://manned.org/xargs.
				[38;2;255;66;69m
	Warning: *Check the list on its own first. Feeding a wrong list into a delete cannot be undone.*
				[0m


		- Delete every file that was found:
		find . -name "*.log" | xargs rm

		- Count the lines in every text file found:
		find . -name "*.txt" | xargs wc -l
