

	awk
	
	Picks out columns of text and does small sums with them.
	More information: https://manned.org/awk.


		- Show the first column of every line:
		awk '{print $1}' file.txt

		- Show the first and third columns:
		awk '{print $1, $3}' file.txt

		- Add up all the numbers in a column:
		awk '{sum += $1} END {print sum}' file.txt
