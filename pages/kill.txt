

	kill
	
	Closes a program that will not close by itself.
	More information: https://manned.org/kill.
				[38;2;255;66;69m
	Warning: *Forcing a close throws away anything unsaved. Ask politely first.*
				[0m


		- Ask a program to close politely:
		kill 1234

		- Force it to close:
		kill -9 1234


	 The number is the program number. Find it with the "ps" page.
