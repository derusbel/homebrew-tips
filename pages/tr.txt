

	tr
	
	Swaps letters or characters for others.
	More information: https://manned.org/tr.


		- Turn everything into capital letters:
		tr a-z A-Z

		- Turn spaces into new lines:
		tr ' ' '\n'

		- Delete every number:
		tr -d 0-9


	 It reads what you type, so it is usually fed by another command with the "|" sign.
