

	open
	
	Opens a file or folder the normal way, with your usual apps.
	More information: https://manned.org/open.


		- Open a file with the app that usually opens it:
		open file.pdf

		- Show the folder you are in, in a window:
		open .

		- Open a website:
		open https://example.com

		- Open a file with a chosen app:
		open -a "TextEdit" file.txt
