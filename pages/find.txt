

	find
	
	Looks for files and folders by their name.
	More information: https://manned.org/find.


		- Find a file by its exact name:
		find . -name "notes.txt"

		- Find every photo, whatever it is called:
		find . -name "*.jpg"

		- Find folders only:
		find . -type d -name "photos"

		- Search a specific folder:
		find path/to/folder -name "notes.txt"


	 The dot means "start looking right here".
