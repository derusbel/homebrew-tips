

	vim
	
	A powerful text editor, right in the terminal.
	Famously hard to leave.
	More information: https://manned.org/vim.


		- Open or create a file:
		vim file.txt

		- Open a file at line 10:
		vim +10 file.txt


	 Press "i" to start typing. To leave, press "escape", then type :wq to save and quit, or :q! to quit without saving.
