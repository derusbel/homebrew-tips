

	du
	
	Tells you how much space folders are taking up.
	More information: https://manned.org/du.


		- See the size of this folder in a readable way:
		du -sh .

		- See the size of each folder inside:
		du -sh *

		- Find the biggest folders, largest first:
		du -sh * | sort -hr


	 The "h" stands for human, so you get MB and GB instead of huge numbers.
