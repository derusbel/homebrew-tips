

	gzip
	
	Squeezes a single file to make it smaller.
	More information: https://manned.org/gzip.


		- Squeeze a file:
		gzip file.txt

		- Unsqueeze it again:
		gunzip file.txt.gz

		- Squeeze it but keep the original too:
		gzip -k file.txt


	 For a bundle of many files, see the "tar" or "zip" pages.
